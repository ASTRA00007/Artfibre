Mahesh Sarkar

Founder & Lead Artist

For Mahesh Sarkar, art has always been more than a profession — it’s a way of seeing, interpreting and expressing the world.

With over 20 years of experience across drawing, painting and tattooing, he brings a thoughtful artistic approach to every piece he creates. Since founding Artfibre in 2016, he has built a space where traditional art and tattoo culture exist side by side.

His work ranges from detailed black and grey realism to Japanese-inspired compositions and fine-line designs. Each tattoo begins with an idea and develops through careful observation, creativity and a personal understanding of the individual wearing it.

Beyond tattooing, Mahesh continues to explore his creativity through painting, drawing and other forms of visual art.

Dhurba Jyoti Baruah

Tattoo Artist · Artfibre Studio

Dhurba Jyoti Baruah is a tattoo artist at Artfibre, with a creative journey shaped by years of practice, observation and artistic exploration. Having trained under Mahesh since 2018, he has developed his own understanding of tattooing, combining technical precision with a thoughtful approach to design.

His work reflects a growing artistic identity, built on patience, attention to detail and a genuine passion for the craft.
